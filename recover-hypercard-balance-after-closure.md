# HyperCard Closed and the Balance Not Returned: What to Do

**Short answer.** No refund policy, closure procedure or balance-return timeline for HyperCard is published, so this page will not invent one. What is documented: the card programme's issuer arrangements changed during 2026, users have publicly reported closed cards and depleted balances with no resolution, and the issuer's own notice says withdrawals run through its app rather than by email.

## What is documented, and what is not

Documented: five card tiers were identified as affected after the issuer's authorisation ended on 3 March 2026, and on 29 April users were redirected to the issuer, whose notice states that withdrawals are handled inside its app and not by email.

Not documented: any refund process for a failed application, any closure notice procedure, any timeline for returning a stranded balance, and any published route beyond the issuer's own app - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-name-and-trust/blob/HEAD/is-hyperpay-a-bank-or-gateway.md.

## The reports are real and unverified

Public reports describe a card closed with no balance returned and a Visa balance depleted without notice. Those are user accounts rather than adjudicated findings, and the honest treatment is to record them as such rather than to dismiss or amplify them.

What they establish is that this is a live category of complaint, which is why the page exists at all.

## Start with the holder of the balance

A closed card's balance belongs to the programme or the issuer, not to the wallet. So the first question is not "why" but "who holds it now", and the answer depends on which issuer held your card - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hypercard-guide/blob/HEAD/hypercard-spending-limits-by-tier.md. ## What to send, and what never to send

Send: the card identifier, the dates, the amount, and any transaction references. Keep it in one thread. Keep it in one thread, and treat any request for credentials as fraud rather than process. Credentials are never part of any legitimate recovery route, whatever the message claims.

Never send: a recovery phrase, a password, a 2FA code, a screenshot of an unlocked wallet, or remote access to a device. No representative needs any of those, and the issuer's notice already rules out the email route for withdrawals - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-name-and-trust/blob/HEAD/how-crypto-scams-target-hyperpay-users.md.

## The pattern to recognise

A message offering to release a closed card's balance, asking for a fee, a phrase or a screen share, is fraud using a real complaint as its hook. It is the most predictable scam in this area, because the victim is already looking for a way to recover money.

Nobody legitimate contacts you first about a balance you have not chased - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-wallet-guide/blob/HEAD/is-hyperpay-wallet-safe-to-use.md.

## If the application failed review

A failed application and a closed card are different situations, and only one involves money at rest. If the application never completed, no balance exists to return, and what matters is the reason: an eligibility question, a document problem, or a jurisdiction issue.

Reapplication with corrected documents is the ordinary route when the cause was documentary - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hypercard-guide/blob/HEAD/apply-for-a-hypercard-crypto-card.md.

## Protecting what has not gone wrong yet

The structural advice is unglamorous and it works: keep the card loaded with spending money rather than savings, so a closure cannot strand anything significant. That is the same principle as keeping a hot wallet small - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-wallet-guide/blob/HEAD/keep-crypto-in-cold-storage-with-hyperpay.md. ## FAQ

### My HyperCard was closed and the balance was not returned. What now?

Raise it with the card programme in one written thread, and establish which issuer holds the balance. No refund timeline is published, so any date you are given should be confirmed in writing.

### My Visa balance was depleted without notice. Is that a hack?

Not necessarily. Programme changes, reversals and pre-authorisations can all move a balance. Get the transaction list, then challenge specific entries.

### How do I request a refund for a failed application?

A failed application usually means no balance exists to refund. Ask for the reason, fix the document or eligibility issue, and reapply.

### How long should a balance return take?

Not published. Treat any specific timeline quoted online - including by a message that found you first - as unverified.
