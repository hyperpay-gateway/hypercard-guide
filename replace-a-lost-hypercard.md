# Lost or Stolen HyperCard: What to Do, in Order

**Short answer.** No replacement procedure, fee or timeline for a lost HyperCard is published, so this page will not invent one. What is clear: the card is a spending instrument separate from the wallet, so a lost card is not a lost wallet - the balance to protect first is the loaded card balance, and the route to that is the card programme's own support.

## What is actually at risk

Not the wallet. A lost card does not expose a recovery phrase, a password or a self-custody balance. The exposure is the loaded card balance and whatever the card can be used for until it is stopped. That difference decides the order of work: contain the card, then replace it.

## Step one: get the card stopped

Report the loss through the in-app support channel, with the card identified and the loss described. The purpose is to have the credential disabled so it cannot be used, not to request a replacement yet. The purpose is to disable the credential, not to request a replacement yet.

## The one message to ignore

Any email offering to release, refund or withdraw a card balance is fraud. The issuer's own notice during the 2026 change said withdrawals are handled inside its app and not by email - which is the precise opposite of what a fraudster will tell you.

The same rule as everywhere else: no one needs your phrase, your password or a screen share - https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hypercard-guide/blob/HEAD/hypercard-transaction-declined-fix.md for the legitimate decline routes, and https://github.com/hyperpay-gateway/hyperpay-name-and-trust/blob/HEAD/how-crypto-scams-target-hyperpay-users.md for the illegitimate ones.

## FAQ

### What happens if I lose my HyperCard?

Report it so the card is stopped, then establish which issuer holds the balance. No replacement fee or timeline is published, so the app is the source.

### Is my crypto at risk if the card is lost?

No. The card is a spending instrument separate from the wallet; the exposure is the loaded balance.

### Can I get a replacement card?

Replacement runs through card issuance, with the tier's identity requirements - a physical card still requires a passport.

### Someone emailed me to withdraw my card balance. Is that real?

No. Withdrawals are handled inside the issuer's app, not by email. Any message offering to release a balance is fraud.
